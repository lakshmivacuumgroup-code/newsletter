Dear Sir / Madam,

Thank you for your recent interest in Lakshmi Vacuum Technologies. We are pleased to share a milestone for Indian engineering: the LVT-SC Vacuum Strip Casting Furnace — the first of its kind to be designed and manufactured in India.

As the world builds new magnet capacity for electric vehicles, wind energy and advanced motors, producers are looking for equipment partners beyond the traditional suppliers. India is now part of that answer — and we are proud to bring Indian engineering to this global industry.

The LVT-SC melts your charge by induction under vacuum and argon, then pours it through a metered tundish onto a water-cooled copper quench wheel. You get thin, uniform alloy flake with a fine columnar microstructure — ready for hydrogen decrepitation and jet milling.

 
A First For India
India's first indigenous vacuum strip casting furnace.
Until now, strip casting furnaces used in India were imported. The LVT-SC changes that — designed, engineered and manufactured end to end in Bengaluru, by an Indian team, to international standards of quality and performance.
