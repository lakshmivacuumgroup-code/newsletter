Dear Sir / Madam,

Most vacuum furnace buyers start with the wrong question. They ask: what size, what price? The right question is: what am I putting inside it?

A wrong selection rarely shows up on day one. It shows up months later as rejections, rework and lost production days. So we wrote a buyer’s guide, Which Vacuum Furnace Do You Need, and Why Your Process Should Decide.

We build vacuum furnaces in Bengaluru and also run vacuum heat treatment across 9 centres in India, so we see both sides: how a furnace is built, and how it behaves on the shop floor every day.

Which one are you?
A · HARDENING
Tool steels, dies and moulds
Ask how fast it cools your steel and how even the temperature is across the batch.
B · HIGH-VALUE PARTS
Aerospace, titanium, high-alloy
Ask how clean the hot zone is and what cycle data is recorded for every batch.
C · BRAZING
Brazing assemblies
Ask how uniform the temperature is and how repeatable each cycle is.
D · SINTERING
MIM, carbide, ceramics, magnets
Ask how debinding and atmosphere are controlled for your material.
