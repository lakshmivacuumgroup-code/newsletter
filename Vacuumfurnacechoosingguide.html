Buyer’s Guide

Which Vacuum Furnace Do You Need, and Why Your Process Should Decide

Most buyers start with size and price. We start with one question: what are you putting inside the furnace?

A wrong selection rarely shows up on day one. It shows up months later as:
→ Hardness not reached, or parts distorting
→ Rejections, rework and customer complaints
→ A furnace too small to keep up, or too large to run economically
→ Production days lost waiting for service

Which one are you?

Each process needs a different furnace.
A Tool steels, dies and moulds
 
B Aerospace, titanium and high-alloy parts
 
C Brazing assemblies
 
D Sintering (MIM, carbide, ceramics, magnets)
 
E Rare earth and alloy casting

Our guide shows what to ask for each process, and what goes wrong if the choice is wrong.
