Dear Sir / Madam,

Strip casting makes the alloy feedstock for NdFeB magnets in EV motors, the thin ribbon for amorphous metal transformer cores, the Sm-Co alloy for aerospace magnets, and the soft magnetic materials used in semiconductor power electronics.

But if you're building capacity outside China, you face five real problems:

01
Supply Chain Dependency
China made 94% of global sintered permanent magnets in 2024. Most strip casters are also imported.
02
Quality Inconsistency
Bad strip = bad magnets. Soft iron, coarse grains, uneven composition. Wasted batches.
03
Heavy Rare Earth Cost
Dy and Tb are expensive. Poor strip needs more. Better strip needs less. Direct bottom-line impact.
04
Equipment Dependency
Imported furnace = 6-12 month lead time, no local service, spares cross borders.
05
Scale-Up Gap
R&D from one supplier, production from another. Different recipes, different quality.
THE TECHNICAL REASON
Why strip casting works
When alloy freezes in milliseconds on a spinning copper wheel, crystals stay small and uniform, soft iron is suppressed, composition is consistent. Vacuum keeps oxygen out. Not a preference — a requirement for automotive and aerospace grade magnets.
